## Assumptions (locked by the brief)

promo · 45–60 s · 16:9 YouTube · narrated by Koe (`en-US-AndrewNeural`, generated
by the live instance) · automation, no review stops · no music source available,
so the film is delivered narrated-only and says so in the delivery note.
