**Build step** — `assets/audio/bed.wav` is generated, not tracked:
run `python3 tools/mix_bed.py` from this folder to rebuild it before rendering.

**Voiceover** — generated by **Koe** itself (`en-US-AndrewNeural`, rate 1.05),
one MP3 per script line in `assets/audio/01–11.mp3`.

**Whooshes** — synthesized from seeded noise in the project's off-line mix step;
no third-party samples, nothing to license.

**Mix** — `assets/audio/bed.wav` is the bed ducked against the real voice
envelope (−8.5 dB under speech, swelling back **+6.5 dB in every gap**) with the
ten cut whooshes already placed, so the render stays frame-exact. Levels: bed
−31.4 dBFS RMS under the voice, −24.9 dBFS RMS in the gaps, ≈11 dB beneath a
voice that sits near −20.5. Rebuild it with `python3 tools/mix_bed.py`.
